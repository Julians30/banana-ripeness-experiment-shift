## Interpretation constraints

- S1 contains post-hoc external performance for all candidate models. External winners are descriptive and are **not** leakage-safe selected models.
- S5 and S6 use observation-level bootstrap conditional on the held-out experiment; they do not provide population-over-experiment inference.
- S7/S8 are sensitivity analyses and do not replace the confirmatory NB04 evaluation.
- S9/S10/S11 use NB06-v1.1 as the authoritative final calibration analysis.
- `external_support_distance_ratio` from NB02 is intentionally excluded from manuscript evidence because exact duplicate Spectral vectors made its denominator zero in several folds.
